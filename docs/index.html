How we separate signal from noise

Growth vs the market

Raw growth misleads. Each problem is compared with all complaints (credit reporting excluded, about 6M a year).

Spread

If one company is more than 40% of complaints, it's a story for regulators, not a market.

Lasting, not one-off

"Fees I didn't expect" counts more than "account frozen during a system change."

Relevance to low- and middle-income families

Payday loans, benefit cards, FHA and USDA mortgages, money transfers, and medical or rental debt score highest.

A buyer exists

A matching open grant, employer, lender or agency. No grant can also mean an open market.
