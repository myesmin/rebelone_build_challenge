### Veterans Losing Ground on VA Home Loans: Escrow Shocks and Stalled Loss Mitigation
**Verdict: Pursue.** The pain is structural, spread across 210 companies, and centers on escrow shocks and missing help navigating hardship options, both fixable with software plus service, and HUD funds the counseling channel.

**What's happening:** VA mortgage complaints rose from 2,372 to 3,923 (1.65x), modestly ahead of the 1.47x baseline. Payment-process trouble leads (1,820), with **escrow/taxes/insurance the largest sub-issue (728)**. Struggling-to-pay complaints (1,025) center on failing to reach the servicer about modification, forbearance or foreclosure (487). Across issues, roughly 1,100+ complaints are "trying to communicate with the company."

**Who's hurting:** Veteran and military families, often first-time buyers who used VA's zero-down benefit. Concentration in TX, FL, GA and VA suggests base-adjacent and Sun Belt households. *Inference:* with little equity and E-4 to E-7 or early-civilian incomes, a $300/month escrow jump or a job gap after separation quickly becomes delinquency.

**Root cause (inferred):** Homeowners-insurance premiums and property taxes are rising sharply in TX and FL, triggering escrow shortages. VA's VASP loss-mitigation program wound down in 2025. The replacement partial-claim authority (2025 legislation) is still being implemented, so servicers like Freedom, Onity/Ocwen and Shellpoint have fewer tools and overloaded queues in the meantime.

**Today's workaround:** Repeated servicer calls, CFPB complaints, payday or credit-card borrowing to cover escrow shortages, or calling a congressman. Free HUD counselors exist but are hard to find and rarely VA-specialized.

**Venture ideas**
1. **EscrowShield**: Monitors a household's insurance renewal and tax assessment, forecasts the escrow shortage months ahead, re-shops homeowners coverage, and files tax-assessment appeals. The family uses it free. Revenue comes from insurance broker commissions, plus a white-label fee from military-serving credit unions that want fewer delinquencies.
2. **Muster Point**: A VA-specialized, HUD-approved digital counseling agency. It handles intake, packages the loss-mitigation file, and logs every servicer contact as an evidence trail. Revenue: HUD per-client counseling funds, state veterans-agency contracts, and per-completed-workout fees from servicers, which save heavily versus foreclosure.

**Why now:** VASP's end and the partial-claim rollout create a multi-year navigation gap. Insurance repricing in TX and FL is ongoing. **HSG-2600-DC-0033** (HUD Comprehensive Housing Counseling) is a direct funding fit. **FOA-VETS-27-01, O-BJA-2026-172760 and -172714** are weak matches covering homelessness and justice, not mortgages. At most they offer partner referral channels.

**Noise check:**
- **Single-company spike: pass.** The top company holds 10%.
- **Short spike: pass.** Escrow and loss-mitigation issues are structural, not an outage.
- **Policy-only fix: partial pass.** VA rules shape the options, but navigation and escrow forecasting are venture-solvable.
- **Catch-all: pass.** There is a clear veteran-homeowner customer.
- **Volume artifact: pass.** Credit-report complaints are only about 181.

**Biggest risk:** Smooth implementation of VA partial claims could shrink the loss-mitigation pain, leaving only escrow as the wedge.

In parallel, call 10 HUD counseling agencies and 3 military credit unions to ask whether VA-default caseloads are rising and whether they would pay per case.
